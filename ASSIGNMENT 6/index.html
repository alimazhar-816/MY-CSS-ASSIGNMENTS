CSS Combinators

1. Descendant Combinator (space)

Selector used: .descendant p

Paragraph directly inside the div (red)

Paragraph inside another div (red)

Paragraph deeper inside a section (red)

2. Child Combinator (>)

Selector used: .child > p

Paragraph directly inside the div (blue)

Paragraph inside another div (not selected)

Another direct paragraph (blue)

3. Adjacent Sibling Combinator (+)

Selector used: .adjacent h3 + p

Heading 3

This paragraph comes right after the h3 (yellow background)

This paragraph is second so it is not selected

This paragraph is also not selected

4. General Sibling Combinator (~)

Selector used: .general h3 ~ p

This paragraph is before the h3 so it is not selected

Heading 3

First paragraph after the h3 (green)

Second paragraph after the h3 (green)

Paragraph inside a div, not a sibling of h3 (not selected)

Third paragraph after the h3 (green)
